Our Services

Comprehensive oilfield solutions for the upstream energy industry

Comprehensive Energy Solutions

At our core, we are committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and innovative energy solutions across the oil and gas value chain.

Electric Wireline Services

Cased & Open Hole Operations

At Priniu Energy, we deliver reliable and precise Cased and Open Hole Electric Wireline Services designed to support safe, efficient, and successful well operations.

With cutting-edge tools, experienced engineers, and a commitment to operational excellence, we provide accurate downhole data that empowers our clients to make informed decisions.

  • Advanced logging for formation evaluation
  • Real-time data acquisition and interpretation
  • Cement bond evaluation and casing integrity checks
  • Perforating and production enhancement services
  • Completion support in both cased and open hole environments

Slickline Services

Well Intervention & Maintenance

Our Slickline Services are designed to deliver safe, precise, and efficient well interventions that support optimal well performance throughout the production lifecycle.

  • Routine well maintenance (gauge cutting, paraffin removal, drift runs)
  • Setting and retrieval of downhole tools
  • Wellbore cleanout and fishing operations
  • Bottom-hole pressure and temperature surveys
  • Memory logging and downhole data acquisition
  • LTA Systems

Drilling Technology Services

Advanced Drilling Solutions

We provide advanced Drilling Technology Services designed to optimize well performance, enhance safety, and maximize efficiency.

  • Directional, horizontal, and rotary drilling with wellbore optimization
  • Rig supervision and real-time monitoring
  • Drill string management
  • Safety & Risk Management with rigorous protocols
  • Hazard assessment and well control measures
  • Technology integration with sensors and automation

Completion & Production Services

Optimizing Well Productivity

Our Completion and Production Services are designed to optimize well productivity and ensure smooth, efficient operations from completion to full-scale production.

  • Well completion design and implementation
  • Production optimization and monitoring
  • Workover and well intervention services
  • Flow assurance solutions
  • Artificial lift system installation and maintenance
  • Well testing and commissioning
