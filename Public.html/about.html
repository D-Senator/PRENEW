About Priniu Energy

The fastest growing indigenous Oilfield Services Company in Africa

Company Overview

Priniu Energy International is a fast-growing indigenous oilfield services company providing technical expertise, equipment support, and integrated project solutions across Africa.

Established in June 2016, we have grown from a company with a mono-product business line to one providing multiple Oilfield Services in more than five African countries.

Our growth is driven by good corporate governance, pivotal partnerships, excellent client relations, and best-in-class technology. Great emphasis is placed on delivering timely, cost-effective, and high-quality services.

The board of Priniu Energy comprises well-placed individuals with extensive executive experience, manifesting in the emergence of a preferred service provider.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To maintain, as the cornerstone of our business, the health and safety of our employees and the protection of the Environment. We are committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and innovative energy solutions across the oil and gas value chain.

Our Vision

To be the leading Drilling Services Solution provider in our areas of operation. We strive to set the standard for excellence in the upstream oil and gas industry across Africa.
